## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
I propose a simple website called FarmDirect where farmers sell their vegetables and fruits directly to
buyers, without agents or traders in between.

## How it works
1. The farmer logs in and lists a product with its name, quantity and price.
2. The buyer (a household or a bulk buyer such as a hotel or shop) browses the products and places
an order.
3. The system groups orders by area and suggests the shortest delivery route.
4. The system predicts which products will be in demand next week, so farmers can plan what to sell.
   
## How it addresses the problem
1. Farmers set their own price, so the margin that normally goes to middlemen is shared between the
farmer and the buyer.
2. Buyers see the farmer's price directly, so they pay less.
3. Delivery route planning reduces transport cost, and demand prediction reduces wastage.

##Uniqueness
1. Simple enough for small farmers to use (few fields, easy language).
2. Combines marketplace, delivery planning and demand prediction on a single platform.

## Feasibility and Viability

Feasibility:

1. Built only with free and beginner-friendly tools (HTML, Python, Flask, SQLite).
2. Can start as a small pilot in one village or district with a few farmers.

Challenges and risks:

1. Some farmers may find the website difficult to use.
2. Buyers may not trust a new platform.
3. Delivery of fresh produce needs to be quick.
4. Online payment and delivery partners need to be arranged.

Strategies to overcome them:

1. Use a simple layout and the local language (Tamil/Hindi/English).
2. Show ratings and farmer details to build trust.
3. Use nearby collection points and route planning to speed up delivery.
4. Start with cash on delivery or UPI, and tie up with local delivery partners.

## Impact and Benefits

Impact on target audience:

1. Farmers: Get a better price and direct buyers.
2. Consumers: Get fresh produce at a lower price.
3. Bulk buyers: Get a steady and transparent supply.

Benefits:

1. Social: Supports small farmers and rural families.
2. Economic: Higher income for farmers, lower prices for consumers.
3. Environmental: Less food wastage and fewer delivery trips.
